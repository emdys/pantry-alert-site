How it works

  • Pick your state, and optionally your stores. The app then watches new FDA and USDA food recalls for you.
  • Scan the barcodes of food you buy or keep at home. The app checks them against recalls on your phone.
  • If a recall matches, or names one of your stores, you get an alert. You can also turn on alerts for every Class I recall in your state.

Common questions

My store is not listed. That is fine. Stores are optional. You still see every recall in your state, and your scans are still checked.

I did not get an alert. Check that notifications are allowed for Pantry Alert in iOS Settings, and that you saved your state in the app. Focus modes can also silence alerts.

A product has no name. No public database has every barcode. Tap the item in Purchases to type a name. Matching uses the barcode number, so a missing name does not affect recall checks.

How do I delete my data? Open Settings in the app and tap Delete all my data.

Important

Pantry Alert shows recall notices that government agencies publish. It can miss a recall or misread one. A match is a reason to check the label and the official notice, not proof that your product is affected. For a food safety emergency, follow the instructions in the official recall notice and contact a doctor if you feel ill.
