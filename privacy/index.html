Privacy Policy

Pantry Alert, effective November 20, 2026

Pantry Alert tells you when food recalls may affect you. We built it to work with as little of your data as possible. This page explains what stays on your phone, what reaches our server, and who else is involved.

What stays on your phone

  • Your scanned barcodes, your logged purchases, the receipt photos and text you add, and the product names you or the app save.
  • Matching your purchases against recalls happens on your phone.

We never receive these. They are not uploaded, and they are erased from your phone when you delete the app or use Delete all my data in Settings.

What reaches our server

When you turn on alerts, the app registers your phone with us. Our server stores:

  • A push notification token from Apple, and whether it is for a test or a live build.
  • The US state you chose.
  • The store chains you picked, and your Class I alert choice.
  • A random install ID that the app creates for you. It is not linked to your name, email, phone number or any account. The app has no accounts.
  • The times your registration was created and last changed.

We use this only to send you recall alerts that fit your state and stores. We do not sell it, share it for advertising, or use it to track you.

Looking up product names

If Look up product names is on (the default), the number of a barcode you scan is sent to our server. Our server asks two public product databases for the name, USDA FoodData Central and Open Food Facts, and returns it to your phone. We do not save the number or the result on our server. Those services receive the request from our server, so they do not see your phone or its internet address. If you turn this setting off in Settings, no barcode numbers are sent anywhere.

Camera and photos

The app asks for camera access to scan barcodes and to photograph receipts. Scans and photos stay on your phone.

Notifications

Alerts are delivered through the Apple Push Notification service. Apple receives your push token as part of normal iOS operation. See Apple's own privacy terms for how it handles that.

Recall information

Recall notices come from public sources, the US Food and Drug Administration and the USDA Food Safety and Inspection Service. Downloading them does not tell anyone who you are.

Where data is kept, and for how long

Our server data is hosted with Supabase in the United States. We keep your registration until you remove it. To remove it, open Settings in the app and tap Delete all my data. That deletes your registration from our server and erases the data on your phone. If you delete the app without doing that, your registration may stay on our server, and it stops being used once Apple reports the token as no longer valid. You can ask us to delete it by contacting us below. Our hosting providers may keep ordinary technical logs, such as request times and network addresses, for security and reliability. We do not use them to identify you.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent personal data.
  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not use analytics or advertising tools, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Children

Pantry Alert is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If a change affects what we collect, we will say so in the app.
